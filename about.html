Who We Are

Our Story

Dahuk was born from a simple but urgent truth too many young people in Bangladesh are suffering in silence online, and not enough people are talking about it.

Our Mission

Dahuk exists to break the silence around cyberbullying and online harassment in Bangladesh. We believe every young person deserves to navigate the internet safely, confidently, and without fear.

Through education, awareness, and accessible resources, we empower students, parents, and communities to recognize, respond to, and prevent digital harm.

Our Vision

"A Bangladesh where every young person can participate in digital life free from harassment, intimidation, and fear — where online spaces are safe, inclusive, and empowering for all."

What We Stand For

Our values guide every decision, every conversation, and every campaign we run.

01

Empathy First
