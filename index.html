Understanding Cyberbullying

Knowledge is the first step toward protection. Learn what cyberbullying looks like, how to spot it, and what to do.

Understand

What Is Cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is the use of digital platforms like social media, messaging apps, or online games to repeatedly harm, harass, or humiliate someone.

It can take many forms: hurtful comments, spreading rumors, sharing private images without consent, or deliberately excluding someone online.

Recognize

Signs to Watch For

Recognizing the signs early can make all the difference. Look for sudden withdrawal from devices, changes in mood after going online, or reluctance to discuss online activity.

If a friend or classmate seems distressed, anxious, or unusually secretive about their phone, it may be time to check in with care and without judgment.

Respond

How to Respond

If you or someone you know is being targeted, know that you have options. Document the harassment, block the person involved, and reach out to a trusted adult or authority.

You do not have to face this alone. Dahuk is here to guide you through every step from understanding your rights to finding the right support.
