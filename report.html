Report an Incident

If you or someone you know is experiencing cyberbullying, you can report it here. Every report is treated with care and confidentiality.

Before You Report

Taking a moment to prepare will help us respond more effectively. Here's what to do first:

Document the evidence

Take screenshots of all harassing messages, posts, or content. Note the platform, date, and time of each incident.

Do not delete anything yet

Even if the content is distressing, preserve it as evidence before blocking or reporting on the platform.

You are safe here

Everything you share in this form is confidential. We will never share your identity without your explicit consent.

What Happens Next?

01

We review your report

Our team reads every report carefully and assesses the situation based on the details you provide.

02

We follow up with you

If you provided an email, we will reach out within 48 hours with guidance, resources, or next steps.

03

We connect you with help

Depending on the severity, we may refer you to legal aid, mental health support, or law enforcement contacts.

Also Report Directly to Platforms & Authorities

Reporting to us is a great first step — but you can also report directly to the platform where the harassment occurred, and to Bangladesh's official cybercrime authorities.
