Organizations That Can Help

These organizations provide support, legal guidance, and advocacy for victims of cyberbullying and online harassment in Bangladesh.

Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission (BTRC)

Government

The national authority for regulating digital communications. You can file complaints about online harassment, illegal content, and cybercrime directly with BTRC.

File a complaint

Cyber Crime Investigation Division (CCID)

Law Enforcement

Bangladesh Police's dedicated unit for investigating cybercrime, including cyberbullying, hacking, and online harassment. File a formal report online or in person.

Report cybercrime

Ain o Salish Kendra (ASK)

Legal Aid

A leading human rights and legal aid organization in Bangladesh offering free legal support to victims of harassment and digital rights violations.

Get legal aid

Bangladesh National Women Lawyers Association (BNWLA)

Women's Rights

Provides legal support and advocacy for women and girls facing online harassment, non-consensual image sharing, and digital abuse.

Seek support

Help Yourself Right Now

Practical steps you can take today to protect yourself and document what's happening.

Document Everything

Take screenshots of all harassing messages, posts, or content. Note the date, time, and platform. Save these in a secure location — they are evidence.

Block & Report on Platform

Use the built-in block and report tools on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or wherever the harassment is happening. This limits further contact and flags the content for review.

Secure Your Accounts

Change your passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and review your privacy settings. Make sure only trusted people can see your posts and contact you.

Tell Someone You Trust

Share what's happening with a parent, teacher, or trusted friend. You don't have to carry this alone. Speaking up is a sign of strength, not weakness.

Take a Break If You Need To

It's okay to step away from social media. Your mental health matters more than staying online. Give yourself permission to disconnect and recover.
